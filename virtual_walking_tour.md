--- 
title: Virtual Walking Tour
--- 

According to Gracie Plata, Tourism Relations Specialist II at the City Tourism Operations Office (CTOO), the introduction of Davao's four new tourism circuits were introduced to highlight the city's multicultural roots. Two of these circuits focus on Davao's historical migrant communities: the Japanese and the Chinese.

Launched in June 2026 under the new *Duaw Davao* Festival, the Chinatown Heritage Walking Tour drew over 200 participants across four consecutive weekends. *Duaw Davao* joins the city's major quarterly fesetivals: *Araw ng Dabaw* (founding anniversary) in March, *Duaw Davao* (visit Davao) in June, *Kadayawan* in August, and *Pasko Fiesta* in December. While the current route covered only one section of Chinatown, the strong turnout signals an interest in Davao's rich heritage. According to Plata, a comprehensive tour of the Chinatown area is currently under development, with an official launch targeted for Chinese New Year 2027.
